About

About

I like to say I took the scenic route in my career.

I started in San Francisco at Activision, took an unexpected detour into motion design for concert tours, then landed in Vancouver at EA Sports, where I grew from designer into one of the organization’s first UX Directors, shaping experiences across FIFA, Madden, NHL, and UFC.

From there, I built a UX team at KIXEYE, moved into healthcare at Providence, designing for patients, caregivers, and clinicians, and eventually returned to EA to lead Experience Design across games and mobile.

Most recently, as Head of Experience Design for skate., I built and led a team of 20+ across UX, UI, and technical design, helping take the product from early development to Early Access and more than 20 million players.

The industries changed, but the challenge stayed remarkably consistent: make complexity feel simple without stripping away what makes a product powerful.

Today, I work at both altitudes: setting product and experience direction, building strong design teams, and staying close enough to customers, data, and the work to make better decisions.

As AI makes execution faster, I think that balance matters even more. Speed is becoming abundant. Judgment, clarity, and knowing what’s worth making are not.
